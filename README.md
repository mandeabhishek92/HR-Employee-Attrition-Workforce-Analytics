# HR-Employee-Attrition-Workforce-Analytics
HR Employee Attrition & Workforce Analytics (HR_Attrition_Analytics_Dashboard.pbix)
A single-page workforce intelligence dashboard designed to help HR leaders identify attrition drivers and retention opportunities.

Key Metrics & Visuals:

Executive KPI Strip: Total Employees, Active Employees, Attrition Count, Attrition Rate (%), Average Age, and Average Tenure (YearsAtCompany).

Dimensional Breakdowns: Attrition by Department, Salary Slab, Gender, Age Group, and Tenure trend line, plus a Job Role & Job Satisfaction matrix and departmental headcount funnel.

Interactive Filtering: Dynamic top-level slicers for Department and Age Group.
